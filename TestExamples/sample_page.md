# Boulder Water Status

Updated by the north pump crew. Treat every reading as provisional until a
second crew confirms it.

## Sources

- **Sunshine Canyon intake** — running. Flow is steady, turbidity low after
  last week's rain. Filters were swapped on the 3rd.
- **Fourmile spring** — running, slow. Expect to wait if more than two
  households draw at once.
- **South creek draw** — closed. Upstream contamination suspected; do not
  drink, do not boil-and-drink. We are waiting on a test from the clinic.

## Treatment

Boiling is enough for the canyon intake and the spring. A rolling boil of one
minute at this elevation is the standing guidance. Chemical treatment is for
water you cannot boil, and the tablets we have on hand expire in the spring.

## Hauling

The cart rotation is posted at the depot. If you take the cart out, sign the
board, and bring it back the same day. Two wheels are bent; the good cart is
the one with the green handle.

## Contact

Leave a note at the depot or flag any crew member wearing a blue band.
