# Roadmap

MOSS grows a little at a time. What's done, what's next, and ideas for later.

## Community pages

1. **Identities and signed pages**: done
2. **Uploading to a Seed Vault, and opening a page by its address**: done
3. **Finding pages** (search, ranking, following, author pages, the editor),
   plus vault specs, spore prints, routing and the dedicated link: done
4. **Locked pages**: pages that need a password to read
5. **Owner tools**: a removal log, a review queue, quiet-hours scheduling

## Ideas for later

- **Searching across vaults**: finding pages that only live on another
  vault, through catalogues that vaults share with each other.
- **Reader + Seed Vault devices**: a desktop with a radio that reads, and
  also serves others at set hours.
- **Page types**: boards and forums (fetch only what's new), status pages
  broadcast once to everyone (signed, unencrypted), maps (base maps
  preloaded, small layers of points over the air).
- **Overheard spores**: readers keeping public broadcast spores they
  overhear.
- **Shared-dictionary compression**, for 10–25% smaller short articles.
- **Signed updates over the air**: new MOSS versions passed along the
  mesh, signed by the project so a radio can't be handed fake code. Useful
  for vaults and readers that never see the internet.
- **Images**: not planned for now. A single photo would be dozens to
  hundreds of spores, and the nopic library has none.

## A principle that won't change

MOSS works with the **standard MeshCore companion firmware**. Nobody should
have to flash a special build to use it.

And MOSS stays off the **Public** channel: it never posts there, not even
to tell people a Seed Vault exists. People find MOSS by word of mouth.
